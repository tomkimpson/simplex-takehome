========================================================================
CONCLUSIONS
========================================================================

1. The KL probe matches the MSE probe on MSE metric while substantially
   outperforming on KL divergence and boundary fidelity.

2. The advantage is most pronounced at deeper layers (layer_2, final)
   where the MSE probe's KL divergence degrades but the KL probe's improves.

3. Under PCA compression (Experiment 2), the KL probe degrades more
   gracefully, confirming the central hypothesis. The advantage is
   largest at k=3-4 (near the intrinsic simplex dimension of 2).

4. Both probes produce valid probability outputs after normalization,
   but the KL probe does so by construction (softmax guarantee).
